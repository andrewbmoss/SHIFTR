Power to Zwift

The power meter reading is multiplied by the scale (1.055 for this Power2Max, from the Saris test). The bridge learns how the trainer relates to it during steady riding; if the meter drops out, Zwift gets the trainer × that factor, and ERG targets are adjusted so the meter shows what Zwift asks for. Chain loss sets the expected trainer factor; drift beyond the warning level means the trainer needs a spindown.
